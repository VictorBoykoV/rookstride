# Rook's connected worlds: artwork

Five original landscape plates extend the existing Rook field journal. Each is a
separate illustration generated with the built-in image generation tool on
14 October 2026. `artwork/rook-harbor.png` was the character and style reference.
The complete final prompts are retained in `artwork/worlds/prompts.json`.

All five masters are 1536 × 1024 PNGs. WebP copies preserve the entire composition
at 1280 × 853, with deployment compression selected to keep each file below
250,000 bytes. Originals remain unchanged. No crops, painted edits, generated
lettering or third-party marks were added during format optimization.

The paintings use warm ink contours, cream paper grain, signal red, charcoal,
ochre and muted landscape colors. A prominent Rook at one edge leaves a broad
vista for readable page composition. These are world covers and scene backdrops,
not fifty individually illustrated chapters. The reader should label reusable
backgrounds and any interactive overlays honestly.

## Reusable helper poses

Six original code-native SVGs in `brand/poses` extend the revised bird mark:
`rook-guide.svg`, `rook-map.svg`, `rook-lantern.svg`, `rook-builder.svg`,
`rook-celebrate.svg` and `rook-wait.svg`. They preserve the exact revision 2 head
paths and add a charcoal scarf, cream satchel and small, purposeful props.
Each includes a title, description and logical classes for optional motion.

The SVGs do not contain executable scripts, external references, fonts or text
rendered into the art. Animation is a presentation-layer choice: honor reduced
motion and provide stage labels independently. A waiting pose is not an estimate
of installation completion. See `../brand/AMBASSADOR.md` for roles and voice.

## Representation and story boundaries

The region-inspired worlds are fictional local places. Their covers are not a
complete portrait of Iran, China, Russia or the United States. Characters keep
their own agency; travelers learn from hosts rather than arriving as saviors of
an entire culture. Obstacles and dragons in fantasy stories must remain distinct
from real ethnic groups, religions and countries.

The art contains no national flags, official uniforms, political emblems,
firearms, copied franchise characters or company mascots. Ambient architectural
details are scenery rather than endorsements or factual historical claims.
Text remains separate, accessible HTML. Narration is an optional reading aid;
it must never play on entry or advance into another chapter without a choice.
