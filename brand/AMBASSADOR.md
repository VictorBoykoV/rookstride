# Rook, our traveling ambassador

Rook is the red bird who makes unfamiliar routes feel approachable. He greets a
reader, asks what they need, shares a map and checks the next bridge with them.
His recurring line is **“A good route leaves the next traveler a choice.”**

He represents the project's habits: curiosity, patience, practical kindness,
open explanations and the courage to start again. He never takes over a reader's
decision. His confidence comes from paying attention, not from knowing everything.

## One character, many useful roles

The small mark remains the clean revision in `rook.svg`. The ambassador adds a
body, charcoal scarf, cream satchel and brass lantern. The satchel carries maps,
books and tools; the lantern means shared understanding. Neither prop is a claim
that the software makes a person invisible or protects every connection.

| Role | Use | Pose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Host | Welcome to the project and choose a starting point | `rook-guide.svg` |
| Navigator | Compare routes and explain architecture | `rook-map.svg` |
| Companion | Read stories, follow a guide, find the next step | `rook-lantern.svg` |
| Builder | Prepare an installation and check its milestones | `rook-builder.svg` |
| Patient helper | Explain a long-running stage without inventing progress | `rook-wait.svg` |
| Celebrant | Acknowledge a completed, verified milestone | `rook-celebrate.svg` |

These original vector poses live in `website/brand/poses`. The head paths are the
same as the revision 2 mark. When adapting them to another format, preserve the
rounded red head, swept crest, projecting beak, white eye patch and dark pupil.
Keep the red, charcoal and warm cream palette. Props may change; the silhouette
and temperament should remain recognizable.

## Where Rook belongs

- Home: one welcoming guide near the first useful action, and a smaller builder
  or navigator beside the installation and architecture sections.
- Download: a guide explains which release and source archive belong together.
- Architecture: a map pose accompanies the route diagram and its real limits.
- Operations: the patient helper appears beside recovery guidance and waits.
- Stories: the lantern introduces each world and invites a reader to begin.
- Installation: a restrained stage pose sits beside the explicit progress text.

A mascot should make a task easier to understand. Keep him out of passwords,
destructive confirmation controls and dense diagnostic output. He does not
interrupt reading, cover a control, demand interaction or replace a status label.
Use one prominent pose per section rather than repeating a large character in
every card.

## Motion and accessibility

Small wing, scarf or lantern movements can acknowledge a change. Slow waiting
motion can give a screen life; it must not look like measured installation
progress. The progress bar, stage name and remaining-step count carry the facts.

Honor reduced-motion preferences. Never flash. Decorative poses have empty alt
text; a pose that conveys useful information needs a nearby textual equivalent.
All six SVGs include a short title and description for standalone use. A user
must be able to read every story without motion or sound. Sound starts only
after an explicit play action, and finishing a chapter does not start another.

## A traveler from the page

Lantern Harbor is the place Rook first remembers. Lumen found him drawn beside
an unfinished route in an old atlas. During a storm, a little lantern oil touched
the margin. The bird blinked and stepped onto the library desk when a stranded
courier needed help. Lumen offered him a name, a scarf and a satchel. Rook chose
how to use them. He remembers the library and his rule, but not an author or a
predetermined destination.

He can now travel through its connected worlds by ordinary journeys—ferries,
caravans, trains and footpaths—and through occasional page-doorways. He remembers
what people teach him and returns their tools. A world is not a national mascot
costume or a complete description of a country: it is an invented setting with
neighbors, difficulties, jokes and many ways of living.

His bravery is cooperative. A princess in a tower can be a skilled astronomer
who helps plan her own rescue. A dragon can be a worried keeper of a bridge who
learns to listen. Rook does not treat a whole people, culture, language or region
as the enemy. He is courteous without deciding who deserves access or respect.

## World adaptations

The five world plates are Lantern Harbor, a Persian-inspired orchard and
caravanserai, a Chinese-inspired canal garden, a Russian-inspired birch village
in winter, and an American-inspired library town near the prairie. They are
fictional local settings, not documentary portraits of entire countries.

Costume serves the story and weather. The winter world adds a plain ushanka and
felt boots without insignia. Other settings keep Rook's ordinary travel clothes.
Do not make clothing, accents or architecture a punchline. No national flags,
party symbols, official uniforms or brand endorsements are part of this system.

Storytelling can suggest listening, checking a crossing, sharing light, repairing
a route or protecting a neighbor's choices. A scene is not evidence about a
protocol, anonymity, regional availability or political history. Technical facts
belong in the documentation; factual historical notes need their own sources.

## Mascot research and design choices

Reviewed 14 October 2026, using official sources. These observations inform
Rook's use; all Rook art and writing remain original.

- Reddit introduces Snoo as a curious community ambassador and keeps its
  recognizable proportions across adaptations. Its mascot guidance separates
  acceptable costumes from changing the core character. We use that general
  principle for a stable Rook head and changing travel props. [Reddit brand](https://redditinc.com/brand),
  [official Snoo guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/204528509-What-are-the-guidelines-for-making-something-with-the-Reddit-mascot-Snoo).
- OpenClaw documents an optional lobster companion that reacts to completed work
  and disconnection, while explicit status remains available independently.
  Its separate lore page gives the symbol a story. For Rook, helper motion is
  optional and the real progress view remains the source of installation state.
  [OpenClaw's companion](https://docs.openclaw.ai/web/lobster),
  [OpenClaw lore](https://docs.openclaw.ai/start/lore).
- NVIDIA's NemoClaw page places named product identity beside concrete features,
  architecture and installation guidance. It is not a published Rook-style
  mascot rulebook. The useful pattern is to make the character accompany clear
  product information, with claims supported by the actual implementation.
  [NVIDIA NemoClaw](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/nemoclaw/).

Rookstride is not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit, OpenClaw or NVIDIA.
Their characters, names and assets have not been incorporated into Rook's art.
