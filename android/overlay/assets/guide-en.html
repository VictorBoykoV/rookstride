Your first VPN, step by step

You need an Internet connection, a reachable fresh VPS and its administrator details. A VPN login is created separately.

Before you begin

  • Android 8 or later and the signed Rookstride APK.
  • Fresh Ubuntu 24.04 on one server, or two distinct servers for the two-hop recipe.
  • Provider address, SSH port, administrator login and password or key. Root or passwordless sudo is required.
  • The SSH fingerprint from your provider’s console, when available.

Install the app

Download the APK and verify its checksum and signing certificate. Android may ask you to allow installation from your download app.

Add your server

Choose Add gateway or Add a two-server gateway. Enter the provider’s details exactly. Keep addresses, usernames and passwords in their original form.

Verify the SSH fingerprint

Compare the presented fingerprint with your provider’s console. A changed key stops the connection. Do not approve a key you cannot identify.

Choose and install the recipe

On a fresh VPS, create your first VPN login and decide whether to keep usage history. The single-server path has five checkpoints; a pair has eight.

Follow progress or keep exploring

The installation service continues while you read Guide or Stories. Tap the progress shortcut or notification to return. Time ranges are estimates, not countdowns.

If the phone stops polling

Detached server jobs may continue. Reopen, inspect and resume the recorded job. A phone force-stop or VPS failure is not a reason to start a fresh install blindly.

Connect on this phone

In People, choose the VPN login and Use on this phone. Enter its VPN password, approve Android’s VPN permission, then connect. The VPN password is not the root/SSH password.

Check the real route

On Wi-Fi and mobile data, check websites, your exit IPv4, DNS filtering and unexpected public IPv6. The gateway provides IPv4 and intentionally blocks IPv6 Internet access.

Invite another person

Create a login, export its .sswan profile and share the VPN password separately through a trusted channel. Invited users can use the official strongSwan Android app.

Starting again is an explicit action.

Reset reviews and deletes recognized gateway data, including users, certificates, filters and history. It keeps Ubuntu, SSH and package binaries. A complete OS rebuild belongs in the provider’s panel.

Type the exact English confirmation shown by the app, such as DELETE VPN or DELETE PAIR. Other clients lose access. Unknown installations are refused.

Know the boundaries

A VPN does not hide GPS, account or cookie signals, guarantee website access or repair the reputation of an exit IP. DNS filtering cannot reliably remove YouTube in-video ads.
