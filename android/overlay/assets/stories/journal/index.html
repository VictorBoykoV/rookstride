THE BIRD BEHIND THE MARK

His map has
room for you.

In Lantern Harbor, every street has a story and every traveler has a different place to be. Rook keeps a folded map in his satchel and a brass lamp at his side.

He isn’t the strongest bird in the city. He’s the one who stops, asks what’s wrong, and helps someone take the next step.

“A good route leaves the next traveler a choice.”

He lends a light. He clears a little noise. And when the first way fails, he is willing to begin again.

Original fiction and AI-generated illustrations. The stories are metaphors for care and curiosity, not promises about VPN behavior.
