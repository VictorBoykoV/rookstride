Lantern Harbor is invented. The other worlds draw on selected gardens, waterways, winter crafts, libraries and cultural traditions. They are not accounts of actual people, events, laws, or the experience of an entire country. Local characters bring the knowledge; Rook listens and helps.

These stories use an accessible English voice. They do not claim to be translated Persian, Chinese, Russian, or regional folklore. The U.S. liberties story names the Second Amendment alongside the wider constitutional tradition; it is fiction, not legal advice. No VPN technology is a guarantee of access or protection.
