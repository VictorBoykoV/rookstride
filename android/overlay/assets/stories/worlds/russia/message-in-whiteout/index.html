The suspended route on the station map

Rook looks toward a distant station lamp through thickening snow while Dasha and a transport coordinator review the suspended delivery route inside the library.

Pip’s letter for Rook arrived at the station as snow thickened. The station courier planned to bring it to the library, but the transport coordinator suspended the route before he left. Rook could see the station lamp across the square and wanted to fetch it himself. Dasha asked what in the envelope could possibly require an unapproved walk through worsening visibility.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 05PAGE 2 / 4
Dasha posting a clear transport update

Travelers settle in a warm library waiting room as Rook arranges chairs and Dasha posts clear transport updates, with the letter safely stored at the station.

The courier confirmed that it was an ordinary personal letter and stored it securely. Rook accepted the delay. Dasha opened a waiting room for travelers whose onward journeys had also paused. Rook helped place clear updates on the board and brought chairs where the coordinator requested them. He could not clear the weather, but he could make an uncertain afternoon a little less lonely.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 05PAGE 3 / 4
Rook pausing Bun’s story

Rook pauses Bun’s story in the waiting room as travelers listen to a transport announcement, with the child’s picture of a round loaf on the table.

A child asked whether everyone would be home by supper. Rook began to say yes, then stopped. “I do not know,” he said. “The coordinator will tell us when the route is ready.” He offered a story while they waited, and the child chose the tale of Bun. Halfway through, the announcement changed. Rook paused the story so people could hear the information that mattered more.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 05PAGE 4 / 4
Pip’s mint-garden drawing

Rook finally reads Pip’s mint-garden drawing at a library desk while a courier signs the ordinary delivery record and snow clears beyond the window.

When the coordinator reopened the route, the courier delivered Pip’s letter through the normal service. It contained a drawing of Fern’s new mint bed and no crisis at all. Rook read it twice, glad to receive it safely. He sent Pip a reply about the waiting room and the child’s question. The friendship had survived an extra day without requiring anyone to become a weatherproof hero.
