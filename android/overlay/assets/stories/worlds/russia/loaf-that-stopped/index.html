The loaf that chose to stop

A familiar story can leave room for a new decision

Bun on the bakery windowsill

A small original talking loaf named Bun blinks on Misha’s bakery windowsill while Rook watches the snowy lane beyond the open window.

Misha the baker set a round loaf on the windowsill, and in this story the loaf promptly blinked. He named himself Bun and rolled toward the lane, singing a song he had just invented. Rook recognized the shape of a traveling folktale, but Bun’s words were his own. “Where will you go?” Rook asked. “Farther,” Bun replied, which was not quite an address.

Rook considering the snowy lane

Bun pauses on Dasha’s tray beside the bakery yard as Rook points out a steep lane and warm light shines from the shop.

Bun rolled safely into the cleared yard, then saw a steep lane ahead. Rook suggested stopping to decide, but Bun worried that every good traveling story required more traveling. Dasha arrived with a tray. “A book may have an ending,” she said, “but you can make a choice before reaching it.” Bun rested on the tray and listened to the quiet inside the bakery.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 02PAGE 3 / 4
Dasha’s secure traveling basket

Dasha carries Bun in a secure basket toward the library as Rook walks beside her and Misha waves from the bakery door.

Misha asked whether Bun wanted to return, stay in the library window, or travel later with a companion. Bun chose a library visit first. Dasha carried him in a safe basket, and Rook walked beside her. They did not sing another storyteller’s refrain. Bun composed a new verse about asking where the next road led and being allowed to change his mind.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 02PAGE 4 / 4
Rook’s reviewed Bun story

Bun sings from a library table before choosing to return home; Rook adds the word chose to the reviewed title while Dasha smiles.

At the library, Bun became the day’s smallest storyteller. After his song, he asked to return to Misha’s warm windowsill. Nobody called the trip a failure because it ended nearby. Rook wrote an original tale about a loaf who stopped, asked, visited, and went home. Bun reviewed the title and added one word: chose. It was his favorite part of the adventure.
