A fictional winter journey inspired by Russia’s varied landscapes and storytelling traditions / TEN CHAPTERS

The Snowbound Library

Rook visits one invented northern community during winter, not an entire country frozen into one season. Dasha’s library, Anya’s workshop, Misha’s bakery, and a passenger railway hold stories of hospitality, care, and honest routes. An original runaway-loaf adventure nods to the broader folktale tradition.

Travel note: A train brings Rook to the winter library; a checked coastal passenger ship carries him west toward the Wide-Sky Commons. · All worlds
