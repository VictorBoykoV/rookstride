The closed lookout tower above the thaw

Rook revises an eager journal introduction beside a closed path notice while Anya’s team examines the thaw-affected tower approach from a permitted work area.

A small village had invited Rook to see its wooden lookout tower. Overnight a thaw changed the approach path, and Anya’s team closed the visit pending inspection. Rook had already written an excited introduction in his journal. He hated crossing it out. Dasha handed him a new sheet. “A changed plan can have a true introduction too,” she said. Rook asked what the visitors could safely do instead.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 07PAGE 2 / 4
The permitted tower photographs

Neighbors share permitted tower photographs and summer and autumn stories in a warm community workshop while Rook listens beside Dasha.

The hosts moved their welcome to the community workshop. They brought photographs they had permission to share and stories from the tower keepers. Rook expected everyone to sound disappointed. Instead a neighbor told a funny tale about a crow who stole a picnic napkin there one summer. Another described a autumn visit. Winter was only the season Rook happened to be seeing.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 07PAGE 3 / 4
Rook’s honest account of the changed welcome

Rook dates permitted tower images and adds Anya’s current closure note, then sketches the workshop gathering while leaving his tower-visit page honestly incomplete.

Rook asked before adding the accounts to his atlas. Anya included the closure note so future readers would not mistake old pictures for a present invitation. They set the dates beside each image. Rook drew the workshop gathering as the day’s actual scene. His unfinished tower sketch remained unfinished, and the empty space no longer looked like something he needed to hide.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 07PAGE 4 / 4
The workshop friends in Rook’s mailed drawing

Rook receives a later tower update during his onward journey and mails Dasha a drawing of the completed workshop welcome with a small crossed-out tower sketch.

The hosts sent him a later update when their inspection was finished. He was already traveling onward, so he thanked them and saved it for another visit. The workshop story had a complete ending of its own: a welcome kept its warmth after its location changed. Rook mailed Dasha a copy with a tiny crossed-out tower and a very large table of friends.
