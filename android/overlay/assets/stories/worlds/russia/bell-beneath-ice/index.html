The bell visible beneath the ice

Lev, Dasha, and Rook look at a small delivery bell visible beneath clear ice near a marked riverbank, with everyone remaining safely on shore.

A dog named Lev lost the library delivery bell beside the river. Through the clear edge of the ice, they could see it near the bank. Rook’s first thought was that a small bird could make a very small step. Dasha asked him to stay on the marked shore. They did not know the ice’s condition, and a visible object did not make the route to it safe.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 03PAGE 2 / 4
Lev receiving the borrowed hand chime

Rook gives Lev an approved borrowed hand chime while Dasha contacts the riverside caretakers and marks the lost bell on a shore map.

Lev needed a way to announce deliveries that morning. Rook lent him a small hand chime from the library after checking with Dasha. The bell could wait. Dasha contacted the people responsible for the riverside area and explained the location. Rook marked it on their public shore map without telling children that the ice was a place to go hunting for lost things.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 03PAGE 3 / 4
The carefully tied delivery parcel

Lev and Rook make deliveries using agreed knocks and the borrowed chime while a caretaker posts a riverbank safety notice in the background.

The caretakers assessed the area and decided the bell should remain until it could be recovered safely from shore. Lev was disappointed, but his deliveries continued. Rook accompanied one trip and discovered that some households preferred a knock to either bell. They updated the agreed delivery notes. An object lying just out of reach had led them to a useful question they could answer immediately.

The Snowbound Library / CHAPTER 03PAGE 4 / 4
Lev receiving his recovered bell

Lev receives the safely recovered bell from the riverside caretaker and returns Dasha’s hand chime while Rook records the patient outcome.

Later, when the caretakers recovered the bell through their own safe process, Lev returned the chime clean and thanked Dasha. He kept the updated delivery notes. Rook recorded the part of the adventure that might otherwise sound unheroic: nobody walked onto uncertain ice, and the letters still arrived. The little bell rang brightly that afternoon, sounding no less lovely for having been allowed to wait.
