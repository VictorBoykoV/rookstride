A fictional world inspired by China’s varied river, craft, and urban traditions / TEN CHAPTERS

The River of Returning Lanterns

A lively river city welcomes Rook among workshops, covered crossings, printers, ferries, and roof gardens. Mei, Lin, and Shen know this place better than their visitor. Old crafts and contemporary city life share the same horizon; this world never claims to represent all of China.

Travel note: A coastal ship brings Rook upriver; a northbound passenger train leads him toward the Snowbound Library. · All worlds
