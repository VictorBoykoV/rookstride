The roof garden

New places can carry old friendships

Mei welcoming Rook to the modern roof garden

An elevator opens onto a contemporary roof garden with planters, a weather board, and a small solar-powered light as Mei and Lin greet surprised Rook.

Mei invited Rook to a community roof garden above a busy apartment building. He had expected every memorable place in the river world to be an old pavilion. Instead an elevator opened onto planters, a weather board, and neighbors talking beside a solar-powered light. Lin laughed at his surprised face. “People keep making places to meet,” she said. “We did not stop when your story began.”

The credited garden sketch

Rook checks planter labels with the building coordinator while a delivery team uses the approved service route and neighbors study Nima’s credited garden sketch.

A planter delivery had arrived at the wrong entrance. Rook offered to carry the heavy boxes upstairs, but the building coordinator had a safe delivery process. He helped check the labels and guide the delivery team to the correct service point instead. The neighbors chose where their new plants would go. Rook shared Nima’s garden sketch as a conversation piece, with its permission and credits intact.

The River of Returning Lanterns / CHAPTER 09PAGE 3 / 4
Neighbors making room at the table

Neighbors rearrange a movable table between a shaded reading corner and children’s drawing space while Rook sketches only agreed details of the roof garden.

One neighbor preferred a shaded reading corner to another row of pots. Another wanted space for children to draw. The garden group found room for both by changing a movable table’s position. Rook asked before sketching the scene and left people out if they wished. The most interesting feature of the roof was not its height. It was how its neighbors kept negotiating a shared place.

The solar-powered light over the garden

Mei and Rook sit under the roof garden’s small light with a permitted photograph and atlas page, while the city’s northbound station glows beyond.

That night the small light glowed above the table, and Mei sent a permitted picture of the garden to Sima. Rook added the roof to his atlas beside the covered bridge, giving both their own dates and stories. Old skills and new meeting places could share a page without becoming the same thing. From the roof, the northbound station shone across the city.
