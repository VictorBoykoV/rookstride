A smaller safe choice can be the joyful one

The paper-boat kite changing direction

Jun and Rook watch a paper-boat kite change direction above a riverside field as a steward observes the crowded walkway nearby.

A young fox named Jun made a kite shaped like a paper boat and invited Rook to its first flight. Rook loved how it rose above the riverside field. Then the wind turned, carrying it toward a crowded walkway. Jun wanted to keep it flying because she had worked all week. Rook remembered the bridge that waited and suggested asking the field steward about the new wind.

The River of Returning Lanterns / CHAPTER 07PAGE 2 / 4
Jun beside the lowered kite

Jun sits beside her safely lowered creased kite while Rook offers quiet company and the steward keeps the field boundary clear.

The steward helped Jun bring the kite down within the field’s safe area. One corner had creased. Jun sat beside it, furious at the lost flight. Rook did not tell her to be grateful for a lesson. He sat with her until she was ready to talk, then asked whether she wanted help carrying it home or time to think about another way to enjoy the afternoon.

The River of Returning Lanterns / CHAPTER 07PAGE 3 / 4
Rook making a tiny paper boat

Jun and Rook make tiny hanging paper boats and an awkward large-beaked bird at Mei’s workshop while the full-sized kite rests safely against a wall.

Jun chose the covered workshop, where Mei had spare paper for visitors. She made a tiny version of the boat kite that could hang over her desk. Rook made a harbor bird that looked comically unlike himself. They laughed at its enormous beak. The larger kite would wait for suitable conditions; the small paper fleet could sail above a table while the wind did whatever it wished outside.

The River of Returning Lanterns / CHAPTER 07PAGE 4 / 4
The repaired kite flying at a safe height

Jun flies her repaired boat kite at a modest height in the open field while Rook watches and a small paper boat peeks from her bag.

Two days later, the steward confirmed that the field was ready for another flight. Jun chose a modest height and brought the kite down before the afternoon changed. She had a fine flight and a fine paper boat at home. Rook put neither above the other in his account. Joy could take more than one shape, including one that fit the day it had been given.
