Mei’s sealed introduction

Mei and Lin give Rook a sealed letter and credited bridge account at a busy northbound station while staff confirm the correct platform.

At the northbound station, Mei handed Rook a letter for Dasha, keeper of the Snowbound Library. Lin gave him a permitted copy of the bridge story, including the apprentice’s correction. Rook checked the train number with the station staff instead of following a painted bird that merely looked familiar. His friends waited with him until the announced platform was ready.

Rook’s secured brass lantern

Rook secures his brass lantern quietly in a passenger carriage as a family settles nearby and the station recedes beyond the window.

A family entered the quiet carriage with a child who was overwhelmed by the station noise. Rook’s lantern handle clinked every time he moved, so he wrapped it with a spare cloth after asking the crew where it belonged. He put his notebook away and enjoyed the view. Being useful on this part of the journey meant taking up a little less sound.

The official rail notice in the carriage

Passengers wait calmly inside a stopped train as Rook points toward the official notice and a boy folds a small paper boat beside the window.

The train paused while the crew waited for a cleared route ahead. Rook’s fellow passengers asked how long it would take. He directed them to the official announcement rather than inventing an answer from the trees outside. A boy wanted a story, so Rook quietly told him about Jun’s paper fleet. The child made a boat from his own scrap paper and kept it on the table.

The River of Returning Lanterns / CHAPTER 10PAGE 4 / 4
Dasha receiving the letter at the snowy station

Dasha welcomes Rook at a snowy station, accepting Mei’s unopened letter while the northbound train prepares to continue beyond the mountains.

Snow appeared far beyond the next mountains. At the announced station, Dasha greeted Rook with an ordinary warm coat and a smile. He handed over Mei’s unopened letter. “I have heard about a library that keeps travelers’ stories,” he said. Dasha took his satchel only after he agreed. “Then you have arrived at a good door.” Behind them, the train carried its other journeys onward.
