The open atlas

In Lumen’s library, Rook notices a red feather in an atlas margin while the librarian supports the book’s loose binding.

Lumen asked Rook to help move the library’s old atlas. Its binding had loosened, and a page caught on his satchel. There, beside an unfinished route, was a tiny red feather drawn in ink. Rook remembered the smell of lantern oil and the first evening he had stood on the desk. “Was I made to finish this map?” he asked quietly.

Lumen the librarian

Lumen speaks beside the open atlas as Pip arrives with handwritten accounts; Rook makes space between tools and unfinished maps.

Lumen set down her tools. “I do not know who drew you,” she said. “I know you came alive when someone needed help. Those are different questions.” Rook looked at the empty spaces. For a moment he wanted a neat answer more than the truth. Then Pip arrived carrying three very untidy stories from the morning’s deliveries. Rook made room on the desk.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 06PAGE 3 / 4
The repaired atlas pocket

A conservator helps mend the atlas while Pip, Moss, and Rook place original accounts and corrections in a new paper pocket.

They repaired the binding with the library conservator’s help and added a pocket for new accounts. Pip’s first story described a route the atlas had missed entirely. Moss added a correction to an old bridge. Rook contributed the sentence he remembered from the margin. None of the additions explained his birth. All of them explained a little more of his life.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 06PAGE 4 / 4
Rook packing the blank leaf

Rook folds a blank atlas leaf into his cream satchel as Lumen leaves the repaired book open beneath a warm reading lamp.

That night Rook left the atlas open, but he no longer waited for it to tell him what to do. Lumen gave him a loose blank leaf for his travels. “Bring questions back,” she said. Rook folded the leaf carefully. His beginning could remain a mystery. Tomorrow he could still choose to listen, be useful, and tell the truth about where his map ended.
