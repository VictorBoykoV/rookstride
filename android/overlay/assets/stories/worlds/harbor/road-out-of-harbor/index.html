Rook packing for the journey

Pip, Moss, and Lumen help Rook pack beside the quay; a caravan meeting note and the library’s return address lie atop his satchel.

On departure morning, Pip packed a letter for Sima and Moss packed far too many pencils. Rook checked the caravan leader’s meeting place before leaving the quay. He could fly across a yard, but not across every mountain and sea. Travel would take companions, rest, and more time than a brave announcement. Lumen slipped the library’s return address into his satchel.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 10PAGE 2 / 4
Aster the patient camel

Rook greets Aster the camel at the edge of the harbor road, securing his lantern carefully while the caravan leader points toward a planned rest stop.

Beyond the harbor, the caravan leader introduced him to Aster the camel. Aster lowered her head until they could speak eye to eye. “I choose a steady pace,” she said. “Passengers who want a race can choose another ride.” Rook agreed. He secured his lantern where it would not swing against her and asked where the others preferred to stop for the night.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 10PAGE 3 / 4
Rook's own travel notebook

At a shaded road marker, Rook writes in his own notebook while Pip’s sealed letter remains in the satchel and Aster waits nearby.

The first hill hid Lantern Harbor, and Rook suddenly missed it. He almost opened Pip’s sealed letter just to hear a familiar voice. Instead he took out his own notebook and wrote what he wanted to tell his friends. Aster waited beside a shaded marker. “Home can travel with you,” she said, “without every private letter becoming yours to read.”

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 10PAGE 4 / 4
Sima's open courtyard gate

Sima welcomes Rook and Aster at a garden courtyard gate with leafy trees beyond the road, accepting Pip’s sealed introduction.

By evening they saw the garden world’s first trees beyond the dry road. Sima met the caravan at the courtyard gate and took Pip’s letter. Rook introduced himself without presenting a list of problems he planned to fix. “I have brought questions,” he said. Sima opened the gate. “Good. We have answers to some, and questions to lend you too.”
