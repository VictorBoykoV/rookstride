Rook’s lit brass lantern beside the quay

Pip waits with undelivered letters beside the dark harbor lighthouse while Rook approaches with his small lit brass lantern on the quay.

The last letters were ready, but the harbor lighthouse was dark. Pip the rabbit courier stood beside the water with his mailbag, watching steps disappear into the night. Rook arrived carrying his small brass lantern. “I cannot see the path,” Pip admitted. Rook looked from the letters to the stone stairway. His lamp could not light the whole harbor, but it could help two travelers begin.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 01PAGE 2 / 4
The empty lantern Pip shows Rook

Pip holds an empty dark lantern at the harbor while Rook listens closely, with quiet buildings and water behind their conversation.

Pip showed Rook his empty lantern. There was no light left inside it, and he did not want to guess his way through the dark. Rook listened before suggesting a route. Together they checked the familiar winding path toward the lighthouse. “Then we will find it together,” Rook said. Pip knew the doors; Rook had a useful lamp. Each brought something the other needed.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 01PAGE 3 / 4
Rook’s lantern on the stone steps

Rook carries the glowing brass lantern up the stone lighthouse steps while Pip follows with his mailbag, both moving carefully along the known path.

They took the stone steps one at a time, keeping to the small circle of gold. Rook carried the lantern ahead, and Pip followed with the letters safe in his bag. At the lighthouse door, Rook passed the light to him. “Keep it,” he said. “Someone else may need you.” Pip accepted it carefully. A useful gift could continue its journey after its first owner stopped walking.

Lantern Harbor / CHAPTER 01PAGE 4 / 4
Pip delivers the last letter at the lighthouse

At sunrise Pip delivers a letter at the bright lighthouse door while Rook waves from the nearby stone bridge above the harbor.

By sunrise the lighthouse shone above the harbor, and Pip delivered the last letter at its door. From the nearby bridge, Rook waved to the courier who now carried a light of his own. Pip waved back before continuing his round. Neither friend had made the night disappear alone. They had shared a route and a lamp, leaving the next traveler two people who could help.
