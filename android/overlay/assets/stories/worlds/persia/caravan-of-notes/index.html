Rook's rigid first schedule

red Rook shows a rigid paper schedule to the caravan leader beside Aster and the shaded caravan yard before departure.

Rook prepared a splendid schedule for the coastal caravan. Every hour had a mark, and every mark looked impressively straight. The caravan leader compared it with the day’s heat, the travelers’ needs, and Aster’s steady pace. “This road is not your page,” she said kindly. Rook folded the schedule and asked how the group made decisions when conditions changed.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 08PAGE 2 / 4
Aster guiding the load adjustment

Caravan travelers distribute shared supplies while Darya keeps her secure letter bag and Rook adjusts a load with Aster’s guidance.

They chose rest points together and checked what each traveler could carry. Darya joined them for part of the route, carrying letters in a secure bag. Rook offered to take it, but she preferred to keep her entrusted work herself. He carried shared supplies instead. Aster approved of the lighter, better-balanced load and let him know when one strap needed adjusting.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 08PAGE 3 / 4
Rook's accurate delay note

The caravan waits in a known sheltered rest court while Rook writes a delay update and the leader observes the hazy road from safety.

A sand-colored haze delayed the next stage. Rook stared at his useless hour marks, then wrote an update for those expecting the caravan. The leader chose a known shelter, and everyone waited there. Darya delivered the update through the agreed contact when it was possible. Rook learned that arriving later with accurate news was kinder than arriving recklessly to protect a timetable.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 08PAGE 4 / 4
The amended coastal route sheet

At the coastal caravan stop, Rook thanks Aster and keeps an amended route sheet while travelers greet waiting relatives beside the sea.

When conditions improved, they continued at the leader’s chosen pace. At the coast, a waiting family greeted their relative with relief rather than complaints about Rook’s schedule. He tore out the hour marks and kept the rest points, contacts, and corrections. Aster leaned down for a farewell. “You have learned to draw a road that can bend,” she said. Rook thanked his very patient teacher.
