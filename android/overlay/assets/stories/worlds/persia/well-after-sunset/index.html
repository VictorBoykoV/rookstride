The well after sunset

Leave difficult work to the people who know it

Nima keeping visitors safely above ground

Rook stands beside a covered well with Nima while Sima directs supper guests toward a neighboring courtyard; the underground access remains secured.

The courtyard’s water delivery failed just before supper. People gathered near the covered well, and Rook wondered whether his small size made him the best person to investigate below. Nima stopped that idea before it became a plan. Underground passages were not adventures for untrained visitors. She called the responsible water team while Sima moved the supper tables toward a neighboring courtyard with a working supply.

The water-supply notice with jug and clock drawings

Rook posts a clear supply notice while volunteers carry sealed water through the courtyard and Nima coordinates with a trained maintenance team.

The water team inspected the problem from their approved work area. Rook helped carry sealed drinking water and wrote clear messages at the library entrance. He asked Nima what information could be shared and kept people’s individual requests off the public board. Families chose whether to collect supplies or ask a volunteer to bring them. Nobody had to explain a private difficulty to a crowd.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 05PAGE 3 / 4
The library lamp above the reading circle

Sima hosts a small reading circle in the library as Rook recounts his bridge mistake and Nima brings a calm maintenance update.

The repair took longer than anyone hoped. Sima opened her reading room and offered stories while they waited. Rook told the tale of the bridge that waited, including the part where he nearly acted too quickly. Nima returned with an update rather than a promise she could not keep. “The team is still checking,” she said. People had time to adjust their evening plans.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 05PAGE 4 / 4
Rook's public contact sketch

Neighbors return borrowed water containers after the repair while Rook records public contact information beside a thank-you note to the water team.

By morning the responsible team restored the supply and confirmed the checks were complete. The neighbors thanked the workers and returned borrowed containers. Rook recorded who to contact next time, leaving out the access details that visitors did not need. The most useful thing he had carried that night was neither a tool nor a heroic idea. It was an accurate message delivered when people needed it.
