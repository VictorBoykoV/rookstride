The princess of dawn

A rescue has more than one author

Soraya at the tower window

Princess astronomer Soraya holds her notes at a tower window while Zephyr curls anxiously around the jammed gate and Rook listens below.

Princess Soraya studied the dawn sky from a tower above the garden. A storm jammed the outer door and frightened the young dragon Zephyr, who curled around the gate outside. Travelers called for a hero with a sword. Rook asked Soraya what she needed. “My caretaker, my notes, and a way to speak to Zephyr without shouting,” she answered from the window.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 06PAGE 2 / 4
Zephyr listening to the courtyard plan

Rook shows Soraya’s courtyard drawing to Zephyr as caretakers wait at a safe distance; the dragon uncurls toward a sheltered garden space.

Rook fetched the caretaker and stood where Zephyr could see him. The dragon expected blame, so he hid his face behind a wing. Soraya lowered a drawing of the sheltered courtyard. “You may rest there while the gate is checked,” she told him. Rook held up the drawing, and Zephyr chose to move. The caretakers could finally approach without putting anyone in danger.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 06PAGE 3 / 4
Soraya carrying her telescope

Soraya exits through the checked doorway with her telescope; Rook protects her dry notes while Zephyr speaks from the sheltered courtyard.

While the caretakers repaired the door, Soraya directed the preservation of her damp sky notes from inside. Rook kept the dry copies under his satchel flap. When the door opened, she stepped out carrying her telescope herself. Zephyr apologized for blocking the gate. “You were frightened,” she said. “Next time, let us know where you need help.” He agreed to practice asking before curling around doors.

The Garden of Many Doors / CHAPTER 06PAGE 4 / 4
The jointly reviewed sky journal

Soraya, Rook, Zephyr, and the caretakers watch dawn from a safe garden terrace beside a telescope and a jointly reviewed story page.

At dawn they watched the sky together. Soraya named the figures she had invented for her notebook, and Zephyr named a cloud after himself. Rook wrote a rescue with no defeated dragon and no silent princess. Soraya checked the account and added the caretakers’ names. “Tell people I asked for help,” she said, “and tell them I helped too.” Rook gladly kept both sentences.
